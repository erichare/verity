## Research preview

Verity provides numerical surface comparisons and calibrated weight-of-evidence
estimates under stated hypotheses and named reference populations. It does not
identify a person, establish guilt, or make a match decision. Outputs are not a
substitute for independent scientific validation or qualified human review.

Preserve uncertainty intervals, scope warnings, refusals, reference provenance,
and diagnostic-only restrictions when interpreting or sharing results. A
diagnostic-only or uncalibrated output must not be presented as validated
casework evidence. A likelihood ratio is not a probability of identity.

## Your responsibilities

Use the service only with data you are authorized to process and disclose. Use
synthetic or appropriately de-identified research scans on the public service.
Do not upload confidential case records or personal identifiers. You remain
responsible for selecting the correct physical mark domain, preserving input
provenance, and verifying that the method and reference population fit your use.

Do not attempt to bypass service limits, interfere with other users, extract
credentials, or use the service unlawfully. The service is intended for adult
researchers and practitioners.

## Availability and limitations

The hosted service is provided as an experimental research preview, as is and
as available. It may refuse a comparison, change, become unavailable, or be
withdrawn. There is no uptime guarantee or promise that results meet a particular
scientific, regulatory, evidentiary, or operational requirement. Keep your own
authorized source data and records rather than relying on temporary artifacts.
